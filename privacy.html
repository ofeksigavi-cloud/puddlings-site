Privacy policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026. Applies to the Puddlings iPhone app and this website.

Short version: Puddlings keeps your drink log on your phone. There is no account, no ads, and we do not sell your data.

What stays on your device

Your drink logs, daily goal, pets, eggs and settings are stored on your iPhone, in a storage area shared between the app, its widgets and its Live Activity. In version 1 there is no Puddlings server and no cloud sync, so we cannot see this information and do not receive a copy.

Deleting the app deletes this data. See how to delete your data.

Apple Health (optional)

If you allow it, Puddlings writes the water you log to Apple Health. That is write-only: the app does not read your other health data. You can turn this off at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings. Health data is never sent to us or to any third party by the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Puddlings schedules reminders on your phone. They are created and delivered locally by iOS. You can change or turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions and purchases

Puddlings Pro is a subscription. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your card or Apple ID details. We use RevenueCat, a third-party service, to check which purchases you own so Pro unlocks correctly and Restore Purchases works. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase information, and acts as our service provider. See its privacy policy for how it handles that information.

Analytics

If you choose to share it, Puddlings collects basic, anonymous usage information, such as whether the app was opened or a feature was used, to help us fix problems and improve the app. It does not include your drink amounts, your Health data or anything that identifies you, and it is never used for advertising or tracking across other apps and websites. You can switch it off in the app settings at any time.

This website

This site has no cookies, no trackers and no forms. Our hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP address and page requested, for security and reliability.

Contacting us

If you email hello@puddlings.app, we see your email address and what you write, and we use it only to reply to you.

Children

Puddlings is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Because your logs live on your device, you control them directly: you can export them to Apple Health, edit them in the app, or delete them by deleting the app. For anything we hold about you (for example an email you sent us), you can ask us to see, correct or delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how we handle your information in a meaningful way, we will say so in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to hello@puddlings.app.
