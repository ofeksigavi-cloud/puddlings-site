Sip. Hatch. Collect.

  1. Sip

    Log water in two taps from a widget or your lock screen. Every sip counts, and fast sips are never scolded.

  2. Hatch

    You start with an egg. Keep logging and it wiggles, cracks and hatches into a new friend.

  3. Collect

    Build a collection of different animals, each with its own little personality.
