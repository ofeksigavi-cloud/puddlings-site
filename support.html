Support

Questions, ideas or a pet that looks lonely? Write to hello@puddlings.app and a person will reply.

Common questions

How do pets hatch?

You start with an egg. As you log drinks over about seven days, the egg wiggles and cracks, then hatches into a pet from the collection. You earn drops for logging, and drops unlock more pets. Every log counts, however fast or slow you drink.

How do I add widgets and use the lock screen?

Home screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap the plus button, search for Puddlings and pick a size.

Lock screen: touch and hold the lock screen, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen, tap Add Widgets and pick Puddlings.

Live Activity: start it from inside the app when you want your pet on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. iOS limits a Live Activity to about eight hours, so start a new one when you like.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Puddlings, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. If Pro still does not unlock, email us and include the Apple ID email on your receipt.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Puddlings and tap Cancel. Your pets stay yours.

How do I delete my data?

Your logs live only on your iPhone, so deleting the app removes them. To delete water you already sent to Apple Health, open the Health app, go to Browse, then Body Measurements or Nutrition, then Water, and choose Show All Data. Puddlings has no account and no server, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Does Puddlings read my health data?

No. If you allow it, the app only writes the water you log to Apple Health. Details are in the privacy policy.

Still stuck?

Email hello@puddlings.app. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened, and we will take it from there.
